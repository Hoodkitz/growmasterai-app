## Email Best Practices

1. **Subject Lines:**
   - Keep under 50 characters
   - Create curiosity
   - Avoid spam triggers
   - Personalize when possible

2. **Body:**
   - Short paragraphs
   - Bullet points for key info
   - One clear CTA
   - Professional but friendly tone

3. **Personalization:**
   - Use their name
   - Mention their products specifically
   - Reference recent company news
   - Show you've done research

4. **Timing:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM (best)
   - Avoid Mondays and Fridays
   - Follow up 2-3 times
   - Space follow-ups 3-4 days apart
