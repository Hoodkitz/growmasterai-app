## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

GrowMaster AI is your intelligent companion for cannabis cultivation. Whether you're a beginner or a pro - our AI-powered app helps you at every step, from germination to harvest.

AI PLANT DIAGNOSIS
Photograph your plant and our AI instantly detects problems: nutrient deficiencies, pests, diseases, and more. You get precise diagnoses with concrete solutions.

AI GROW COACH
Your personal grow advisor is available 24/7. Ask questions about watering, nutrients, lighting, training techniques, and more. The coach knows your plants and gives individual tips.

PLANT MANAGEMENT
Manage all your plants in one place. Track growth phases, document progress, and keep an overview of your entire grow.

GROW JOURNAL
Keep a detailed diary with photos, measurements, and notes. Document every day of your grow and learn from your experiences.

STRAIN DATABASE
Over 1000 cannabis strains with detailed information on THC/CBD content, flowering time, yield, difficulty level, and growing tips.

COMMUNITY
Connect with other growers. Share your successes, ask questions, and learn from the community. With likes, comments, and direct messages.

GAMIFICATION
Collect XP, level up, and unlock achievements. Daily streaks and a leaderboard motivate you to keep going.

MARKETPLACE
Discover seeds, nutrients, equipment, and more from verified vendors. With community reviews and recommendations.

SMART TOOLS
- VPD Calculator
- Nutrient Calculator
- CO2 Calculator
- Grow Calendar with Lunar Phases
- Cost Tracker
- Harvest Forecast

PREMIUM FEATURES
- Advanced AI diagnoses
- Unlimited grow coach
- Manage more plants
- Ad-free experience
- Priority support

The app is developed in Germany and is GDPR-compliant. All data is stored securely.

Note: This app is intended for legal cannabis cultivation only. Please familiarize yourself with the laws applicable in your country.
