## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

GrowMaster AI guides adults through legal home cultivation of plants. Take a photo, spot the problem, get a solution: the AI helps with nutrient deficiencies, pests and climate issues and answers your questions in a chat.

PLANT DIAGNOSIS BY PHOTO
Upload a photo of your plant and optionally describe what you notice. The AI names the likely problem, its severity and concrete next steps.

GROW COACH
Your personal cultivation expert in a chat. Ask about watering, nutrients, light, climate, training or harvest and get answers in plain language.

KNOWLEDGE
A searchable knowledge base with more than 800 entries drawn from cultivation videos, for example on nutrients, pests, climate, growth and harvest. Plus more than 450 strain profiles with growing notes. With sources.

MY PLANTS AND JOURNAL
Manage your plants, follow phase and day, write notes and keep a photo record of your grow.

TOOLS
Moon calendar with a daily tip, VPD calculator, nutrient calculator and more.

COMMUNITY
News and the current legal situation, leaderboard, XP, streaks and achievements. Occasional free prize draws from partners with clear terms.

FREE AND PRO
You can use the app for free. Pro unlocks unlimited diagnoses, coach messages and plants, plus more offline knowledge.

IMPORTANT
For people aged 18 and over only. The app does not sell cannabis and does not facilitate sales. AI answers are hints and no substitute for professional advice. Check the laws of your country. Product recommendations are marked as ads.

Developed in Germany.
